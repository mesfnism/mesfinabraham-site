Services

Research and data work for organisations in Ethiopia and beyond.

I take on defined pieces of work with clear questions, honest limits and written deliverables.

What I do

Monitoring, evaluation and impact assessment

Evaluation design, survey instruments, fieldwork supervision, analysis and reporting. Methods include quasi experimental comparisons and mid term reviews.

Company and source verification

Checking claims, records and sources behind a dataset or a report, and documenting where each figure comes from.

Market research and feasibility studies

Demand, competitor and investment assessments, including B2B research for businesses entering or operating in Ethiopia.

Data cleaning and analysis

Cleaning messy survey and administrative data in Excel, R or Stata, with a log of every change. Qualitative coding is available too.

Education content, tutoring and editing

Economics teaching material, tutoring for university students, and editing of research writing.

How a project runs
  1. We agree the question, the deliverable and the timeline in writing.
  2. I check the available data and say early what it can and cannot answer.
  3. I share a draft for comment before the final report.
  4. The final report states its limits plainly.

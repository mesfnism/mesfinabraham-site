Public evidence tool

EthioSkills

A daily record of what Ethiopian employers advertise, turned into regular reports on the skills, education and sectors in demand.

What it does

EthioSkills collects job postings from more than ten Ethiopian job sites every day. It standardises titles, locations, education and experience requirements, then classifies each posting. Weekly and monthly reports summarise what changed.

What it does not do

Job postings show advertised demand. They do not measure total employment, wages in the whole economy, or jobs filled through personal networks. Informal work is largely invisible in this source. Reports state these limits next to every result.

Who it is for
  • Universities and training institutes planning courses
  • Ministries and agencies tracking the labour market
  • Development partners and employers
  • Researchers studying skills demand

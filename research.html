Research

How schooling spread, and what it changed.

My dissertation follows the expansion of formal education in Ethiopia over more than a century and asks how it links to jobs, structural change and inequality.

Dissertation

Educational Expansion, Labor Markets, and Structural Change in Ethiopia

Wageningen University and Research, promoter Prof. Ewout Frankema

Part of the NWO Vici project South South Divergence. The chapters draw on administrative and archival records, household and census data, and birth cohorts from 1940 to 1980.

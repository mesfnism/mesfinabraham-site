Development economist, PhD candidate at Wageningen University and Research

Evidence on education, work and inequality in Ethiopia.

I study how schooling expanded across Ethiopia and what it did for jobs and living standards. I also run evaluations, market studies and data checks for organisations that need numbers they can defend.

Doctoral dissertation

EthioSkills and EthioGTS

Two public evidence tools I build and maintain. One tracks advertised demand for skills in Ethiopian job postings. The other registers graduate tracer studies. EthioSkills, EthioGTS.

Data periods

Periods covered by the dissertation research. Bars are drawn to one scale from 1900 to 2018.
